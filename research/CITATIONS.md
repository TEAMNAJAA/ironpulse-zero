# CITATIONS.md

Citation and acknowledgement text for every dataset **used** in this study, in the form its owner
asks for. Datasets that were surveyed but not used are listed at the end for completeness.

## 1. MAFAULDA — Machinery Fault Database (UFRJ)

Used for: E1-E7. Primary dataset.

The database page states no formal citation string and no explicit licence. It credits the
Signals, Multimedia and Telecommunications Laboratory, COPPE/Poli, Universidade Federal do Rio de
Janeiro, and names Felipe M. L. Ribeiro (felipe.ribeiro@smt.ufrj.br) as the contact.

**Acknowledgement to use:**

> MAFAULDA — Machinery Fault Database. Signals, Multimedia and Telecommunications Laboratory
> (SMT), COPPE/Poli, Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
> https://www02.smt.ufrj.br/~offshore/mfs/page_01.html
> Data acquired on a SpectraQuest Machinery Fault Simulator (Alignment-Balance-Vibration trainer).

**Note on licence:** because the page carries no explicit licence grant, treat MAFAULDA as
"free for academic use with acknowledgement" and, before any publication or commercial use, email
the contact above to confirm. Do not assume redistribution rights.

Commonly cited alongside the database:

> Ribeiro, F. M. L. et al. *MAFAULDA: Machinery Fault Database.* Signals, Multimedia and
> Telecommunications Laboratory, COPPE/UFRJ.

## 2. CWRU Bearing Data Center

Used for: E1 pipeline-correctness gate, E2-E7 cross-check, E5 cross-machine transfer.

The Bearing Data Center asks that the source be acknowledged. The wording it supplies is:

> The data used in this work was provided by the Case Western Reserve University Bearing Data
> Center, Case School of Engineering.
> https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter

The apparatus and procedure description that accompanies the data:

> Smith, W. A. and Randall, R. B. "Rolling element bearing diagnostics using the Case Western
> Reserve University data: A benchmark study." *Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing*,
> 64-65 (2015) 100-131.

(The Smith and Randall paper is the standard reference for *how to use* the CWRU set correctly,
including which files are known to be problematic. Cite it whenever CWRU results are reported.)

## 3. IMS / NASA Bearing Data (run-to-failure)

Used for: E8 degradation-trend experiment.

The readme distributed inside the archive names the required reference paper:

> Qiu, H., Lee, J., Lin, J. and Yu, G. "Wavelet filter-based weak signature detection method and
> its application on rolling element bearing prognostics." *Journal of Sound and Vibration*,
> 289 (2006) 1066-1090.

> the NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence data repository.
> https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/ims-bearings

**Licence:** US Government work — https://www.usa.gov/government-works

## Software

All analysis used only the packages listed in the task specification, on CPU, with no
deep-learning framework: NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, pandas, Matplotlib, PyYAML, soundfile,
librosa, requests, tqdm.

One tool outside that list was used for extraction only, never for analysis: **7-Zip**
(pre-installed on this machine) to unpack the `.rar` archives inside `IMS.zip`. Noted in
`QUESTIONS.md`.
